# Ars Magna Defluens v0.6 — 15-intent / 14-language development lab

> **Historical scope note:** v0.6 describes the independent WordBench 15 × 14 probe lab. The later 2026-11-05 FR/EN candidate build and qualification applies to the six-operation SA core profile, not to the entire lab. See [Windows validation](WINDOWS_GF_VALIDATION_2026_11_05.md).

## Delivered

The branded **Atelier sémantique** now has two separate, explicit flows:

1. **Canonical five-case SA candidate** (carried forward from v0.5):
   `CommunicationRequest → SA candidate pipeline → exact compiled PGF`.
   This has been observed to generate a real French phrase on Windows.
2. **Individual WordBench practical probe** (added in v0.6): select one of the
   **14 prepared languages** and **15 required intents**, then read the most
   recent batch archive or run precisely that one case with official `gf.exe`.
   This is direct GF development evidence, **not** a SemantiK semantic render.

The new intent mapping comes from SemantiK Architect's public
`defluens-intents` CLI: 12/15 identifiable SA operation families and 3/15
pending contract extensions. No family is declared verified by this map.

The console preserves GF `&+` in raw evidence and joins it only for readable
presentation. One-run JSON export preserves provenance and a clear candidate
status. WordBench writes complete reports under
`%USERPROFILE%\Downloads\Ars_Magna_Defluens\practical_single_previews`.

## Install

Extract the v0.6 **correctif** archive in `C:\mycode` over v0.5 after backup,
then double-click
`C:\mycode\Grammatical-Framework\ars-magna-defluens\Lancer_Ars_Magna_Defluens.bat`.

In **Atelier sémantique**, use the new *Atelier GF — 15 intentions × 14 langues*:
select `Fre` and `factual-negation`, click **Voir la dernière preuve** if you
already have a completed full campaign, or **Exécuter cette sonde avec GF 3.12**
to run the current sources again. Use *Sortie GF brute* to compare token markers.

## Not delivered / not claimed

- No dynamic arbitrary-entity rendering for all 15 intents through SA yet.
- No compilation of one unified 15-intent SA PGF for 14 languages yet.
- No promotion, production readiness, linguistic gold or native review.
- No new grammar rules in Defluens, no new SA semantic planner, no RGL patch.

Next increment: develop canonical SA request/bridge/PGF slices for the
intent families, starting with attribute, intransitive event, directive and
then the three explicitly missing conditional/attribution operations.
